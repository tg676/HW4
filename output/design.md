# Campus Customs: Design (Problem 10)

**Concept: "Varsity Letterman."** The site should feel like pulling on a Yale letterman jacket in a campus co-op. Every visual idea comes from physical college apparel: chenille patches, stitched seams, sweater-cuff stripes, felt pennants, and hangtags. The point is that it reads as *the* Yale shop, not a generic template, and that shoppers trust it and stay.

## What changed, and why it helps people stay and buy

| Area | What we changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | **Graduate**, a collegiate block font, for headings, prices, and size letters. **DM Sans** for body text. **Caveat** handwriting for small "kickers" like *"pick your layer"* and the shopper's name in the nav (*Hi, Test*). | Varsity lettering says "Yale merch" instantly. Clean body text keeps descriptions easy to scan. The handwriting adds a friendly, local-shop voice. |
| **Color** | Yale Blue `#00356b` + deep navy, on **cream "campus paper"** (`#fbf8f2` with a faint grain), with **brass** `#c8a24a` as the single accent (price tags, low-stock, underline). | Warmer and more premium than white. Brass only marks things that need action (price, "only 2 left"), so the eye goes there. |
| **Brand marks** | Chenille-patch "Y" logo with a stitched inner ring (also the favicon). Navbar and footer end in **sweater-cuff stripes**. Cards, forms, and banners have **dashed "stitch" borders**. | Repeating physical details make the site look custom-made, which builds trust for a store selling physical goods. |
| **Hierarchy** | Hero: kicker → huge varsity headline with an animated gold underline on *Loud* → one line of copy → 2 CTAs → **live stats from the DB** (102 styles · 102 in stock · XS–XXL). Every section has a kicker plus a striped section title. | One clear path to "Shop the Collection." Real numbers make it feel like a stocked store. |
| **Hero product presentation** | Three real products as **taped-up polaroids** with handwritten captions ("the classic", "good boy", "beat harvard"). They float gently and straighten and zoom on hover, plus a waving felt **YALE pennant**. Each polaroid links to its product. | Shows actual merch above the fold, with personality, so the first click can go straight to a product. |
| **Shop by Category** | Five navy/blue **chenille-patch tiles** (Hoodies 27, Crewnecks 30, T-Shirts 26, Quarter-Zips 11, Jackets & Fleece 8; live counts) that bounce on hover and deep-link to `/products?cat=…`, pre-filtered. | Most shoppers know the garment they want, so this is one click from the home page to the right grid. |
| **Product cards** | **Brass hangtag price** (with a punched hole) that swings on hover. Garment-type label. **Size strip** under every card: XS–XXL chips, blue = in stock, gold = 1–3 left, struck-through = sold out. A red rubber-stamp **"SOLD OUT"** for fully out items. Cards rise in with a staggered animation, and the image zooms on hover. | Price and **your size's availability are visible before clicking**, so there are fewer disappointing clicks into sold-out items. Gold "low" chips add honest urgency. |
| **Product page** | Stitched photo frame with hangtag, color pills, and **jersey-number size tiles** (big varsity letters; gold *"Only 2!"* for low stock; greyed-out and struck through when sold out). Then **🐾 Ask buttons**: "Is this in medium?", "Show me similar items in stock", "What would go well with this?". These open the chat and send the question with page context. | Stock is impossible to misread. The Ask buttons turn hesitation ("will it fit / is there another?") into an instant answer. |
| **Chat feel** | The launcher is a **chenille "Y" patch** with an **"Ask the Bulldog"** label and a pulsing green online dot. The panel pops open with a springy animation, has a cuff-striped header, a "Y" avatar, and *"Online · answers from live inventory"*. Messages slide in, a **three-dot typing indicator** shows while it works, and chips scroll horizontally. | The assistant has a personality and a promise (live inventory), so more shoppers try it. Motion makes the wait feel shorter. |
| **Motion** | Scrolling announcement **marquee** (store address, licensed apparel, "Ask the Bulldog"). Springy hovers. Staggered card entrances. **`prefers-reduced-motion` turns all of it off.** | Feels alive without blocking anything, and stays accessible for motion-sensitive users. |
| **Forms & toolbar** | Login and signup are stitched cards with focus rings. The products toolbar is frosted-glass, sticky, with pill inputs. Chat search results get a **navy stitched banner**. | Consistent polish everywhere; account creation feels as trustworthy as browsing. |
| **Responsive** | Under 900px: the hero stacks, the collage shrinks, categories go 2-up, size tiles go 3-up, and the chat label hides (just the patch). | Works on phones, where most campus shopping happens. |

## Honesty guardrails in the design
- Everything with a number is **live from the DB**: hero stats, category counts, hangtag prices, size strips, "Only 2!". No fake countdowns, discounts, or "X people viewing" pressure.
- The marquee repeats only true facts (address, licensed apparel, assistant).
